App Store keywords field — 100 character budget
------------------------------------------------
Rules:
  • Never repeat words already in the App Name or Subtitle (Apple indexes
    both; repetition wastes your 100 chars).
  • Comma-separated, NO SPACES after commas.
  • Apple handles plurals and basic stemming — use the singular form.
  • Treat each comma-separated item as a word that can combine with any
    other word to form a two-word phrase. "block,wood,puzzle" ranks for
    "wood block puzzle" too.

Current reserved words (already in name + subtitle):
  App Name: Block Puzzle: Games
  Subtitle: Drop, Fuse, Clear, Combo!
  → Reserved: block, puzzle, games, drop, fuse, clear, combo

Current keywords (99 chars):

Note: "block", "puzzle", "games", "drop", "fuse", "clear", "combo" are already
indexed via app name + subtitle. Keep them out of the keywords field to avoid
wasting the 100-char budget.


Google Play — no keyword field
-------------------------------
Google Play scans the FULL description. The full-description.txt file is
tuned for ~2.5% density on "block puzzle", 1–2 mentions each of secondary
terms, and natural (not spammy) placement. Do NOT keyword-stuff — Play
Protect flags listings with unnatural keyword density.


Competitor benchmarks (as of writing)
-------------------------------------
Top block puzzle apps consistently use:
  • "Block Puzzle" or "Block Blast" as the first two words of the app name
  • A benefit-first subtitle (e.g. "Classic Puzzle Challenge")
  • 4000-char descriptions with heavy use of ★ bullets and short paragraphs
  • Daily-mechanic emphasis (daily puzzle, daily rewards) for retention signals
